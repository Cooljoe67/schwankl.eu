SMIO Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Who we are

SMIO is operated by Marcus Schwankl ("we", "us"). Contact: web@schwankl.eu

2. What data we process

When you sign in to SMIO with a third-party account, we receive only the basic profile information you approve (such as your name and email address) and the access permissions you grant. We use this data solely to provide SMIO's functionality to you.

3. How we use your data

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except where required to operate the service or required by law.

4. Storage and retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the service. You can revoke SMIO's access at any time in your account settings with the sign-in provider.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To exercise your rights, contact us at the address above.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this page.
